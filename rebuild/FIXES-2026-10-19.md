## Storage and compatibility

- DB version increases from 22 to 23 through an additive migration: `group_dispatch`, `group_reads`, `group_read_sent`, recipient/source fields on `group_delivery`, and timestamp indexes. No existing history table is rebuilt or dropped by the new migration. Do not downgrade this DB by installing an older APK over it.
- Pending group jobs remain inside existing SQLCipher encryption. The same inner-envelope encryption and transport are used during background dispatch. Jobs recheck current membership, contact eligibility gates, retention and the continued existence of their source message. Existing outbox source checks prevent deleted queued posts from sending later.
- Group dispatch counts toward pending-outbox/reset waiting and retry wakeups. Existing cleanup also prunes expired staged work and orphan receipt metadata. Seven-day history retention and emergency wipe behavior are not intentionally changed.
- New group read controls use the existing v2 group framing; older peers ignore the unsupported control kind and will not produce group receipts. New exact-ID DM receipts retain the old timestamp prefix; old clients keep their old receipt semantics until updated.
- "Sent" continues to mean server acceptance, not read. Group UI shows acknowledged reader count; a complete read tick requires all recorded original recipients. Legacy messages without a known target snapshot do not falsely become all-seen.
- No new relay, Tor, media hosting, Firebase or server configuration is introduced. No release version/signing change is included.
